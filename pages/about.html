Where Maratha Pride Meets Imperial Luxury

Established in the cultural heart of Pune at Sadashiv Peth, Royal Ethnics by Shashi Creation was conceived with a clear vision: every groom deserves to dress like true royalty on his wedding day.

What sets Royal Ethnics apart is our obsessive dedication to cut, proportion, and textiles. From historic Peshwa-inspired drapes to opulent Mughal Nawabi angrakhas and contemporary Indo-Western jackets, each garment is handcrafted to honor tradition while commanding modern presence.

We take immense pride in our Boys' Royal Collection, giving young princes matching silhouettes with comfortable, gentle linings that let them enjoy festivities without discomfort.

1. Rare Textiles

We hand-select heavy micro velvets, handwoven Varanasi raw silks, and pure Chanderi brocades directly from generational looms across India.

2. Bespoke Fit Studio

Our in-house master tailors conduct detailed posture evaluations, ensuring the chest pad, collar posture, and shoulder line fit flawlessly.

3. Complete Accessories

No royal ensemble is complete without finishing accents. We curate matched turbans (safas), velvet stoles, kalgis, pearl malas, and handmade mojris.
